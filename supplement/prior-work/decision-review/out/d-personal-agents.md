# Further passages

## Reducing the need for people's decisions treated as a goal

- **Claude Duet.** "Approval fatigue is a serious issue, because too many requests teach people to tap through them, sometimes without adequate consideration. We will continue to adjust this calibration over time." ([safety article](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)). Also: "Some of what's described here is still changing: how often it asks for approval, how you sign in, and how well the sensitive-information checks tell what's private from what's harmless." (same article, "Building this with you")
- **Grok Bot.** The changelog repeatedly ships fewer approvals as an improvement: "Auto-review lets through more private actions you can undo without an approval card" (v0.77.0, Oct 26, 2026); "Background work you start in your own words no longer asks for Auto-review approval to start." (v0.77.0); "Auto-review shows fewer unneeded approval cards when its check is slow or the conversation is long." (v0.78.4, Nov 3, 2026); "After you approve a flight, stay, or grocery cart and its total, the Bot pays on its own and asks again only if something material changes." (v0.78.4); "Auto-review falls back to an approval card less often" (v0.75.5, Oct 23, 2026) ([changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot)). Docs: "Most actions run on their own." ([approvals](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy))
- **Muse.** "Your Muse learns over time which decisions need your sign off." ([guidance and approval](https://www.meta.com/help/superintelligence/1385290430137537/))
- **Gemini.** An unneeded hand-off is classed as a mistake: "Asking you to take over when it can normally do the step" ([auto browse](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16821166)). "Save your preference so Gemini doesn't have to ask every time for that action." ([permissions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18766567))
- **Dots.** "Some default action and confirmation rules can be customized, but owner approval cannot override built-in safety requirements." ([security](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/dots-security))

## Passages saying people's decisions should not be reduced

- **Claude Duet.** "Some other steps aren't covered by a "don't ask me again" rule, however often you have approved them, like paying or sending money, deleting an order, or creating an account." ([safety article](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security))
- **Gemini.** "Even if you update your permission preferences, very sensitive actions require your permission each time." and "Your active supervision is the most important way to protect against risk when you assign tasks to Gemini." ([tasks](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507))
- **Grok Bot.** "Avoid broad rules such as "allow everything in the browser." ... Auto Review is model-based and should complement, not replace, least privilege and explicit approval boundaries." and "Keep sending, publishing, purchasing, deletion, and production changes behind approval." ([approvals](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy))
- **Muse.** "Because Muse acts on your behalf, you're responsible for guiding it carefully and approving its actions." and "Keep your default permissions under review to stay in control." ([guidance and approval](https://www.meta.com/help/superintelligence/1385290430137537/))
- **Dots.** "Neither instructions nor custom rules override built-in safety requirements." ([dots](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)); "Disabling custom rules does not force every action to ask for approval." ([security](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/dots-security))

## Other relevant passages

- **Automatic review of actions.** All five have one. Duet: "A separate, automated check runs alongside each action, built on the auto mode classifier from Claude Code and extended for Claude Duet. It assesses whether the action matches your intent." Dots: "Auto-review checks consequential actions before they run". Grok: "Auto Review checks each action your Bot is about to take". Muse: "Many of these approval checks are enforced outside the AI model, so they don't rely only on Muse deciding whether it should ask." ([privacy page](https://www.meta.com/help/superintelligence/1047255454427887/)); "A separate Sentinel agent ... Nothing Muse does reaches the internet unless the Sentinel approves it, and it asks the person for permission when needed." (launch post). Gemini: "Gemini is designed to apply built-in safeguards that confirm some actions with you even if you set a permission to "Always allow."" (permissions page).
- **Grok Bot rule cap.** "If you already have 20 Allow rules, **Always allow** on a card replaces your oldest Allow rule." ([approvals](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy))
- **Grok Bot, team learning review (not about decisions).** "What teammates teach a Team Bot waits under Review learnings until the owner or a Manager approves it." (changelog v1.0.1, Nov 11, 2026). This reviews learned content, not past approvals, so it does not change H3.
- **Grok Bot, advice to users.** "Preserve source links and an action log for important decisions." ([approvals](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy)). The product asks the user to keep the log; it does not define one.
