# Criteria

Each source gets one value for each of four criteria: **yes**, **part**, **no**, or **not assessed** (the material read does not let the rater decide). Every value needs one quoted passage from the source (or, for **no**, a statement of what was searched and not found). A rater decides from what the source states. A rater does not infer what a source "probably" has. When two values are both defensible, the rater gives the lower one and marks the cell DISPUTABLE with the other value and the reason.

The criteria deliberately separate three questions that an earlier grid mixed: whether an organizational area exists, whether it is specified, and whether a record gives accountability and routing.

## C1. An area of work exists in the model
The source's model has a named unit that stands for an area of work or an organizational unit (for example a domain, department, business unit, subdomain, group, organization or capability area), and the unit is distinct from a single agent.
- **yes**: the unit is a defined element of the model (it has a definition or stated properties), and more than one agent or role can belong to it.
- **part**: such units are mentioned or used (for example agents are "assigned to domains"), but the unit is not defined; or the unit is the same thing as one agent or one team of agents built for one task.
- **no**: no such unit.

## C2. The area is specified apart from who does the work
- **yes**: the source specifies, at the level of the area and not per agent, at least (a) what the area is responsible for and (b) roles or positions that agents or people fill.
- **part**: the source specifies some properties at the level of the area (an owner, a scope, capabilities, rules) but not both (a) and (b); or it specifies responsibilities and roles per agent (an agent charter or contract) and not per area.
- **no**: neither.

## C3. A record of accountability
- **yes**: the source describes a record (registry, table, map, catalog) in which each responsibility or capability has one named accountable area or owner.
- **part**: a record names an owner, a provider or a responsible party per agent, asset or capability, but accountability is not stated, not unique, or not per responsibility or capability.
- **no**: no such record.

## C4. Finding where to send work
- **yes**: at run time an agent can look up, from a kind of work or a capability, the agent or role that handles it and how to reach it.
- **part**: agents can discover other agents (a list, agent cards, a registry with endpoints), but the lookup is not from a kind of work; or routing exists only inside one orchestrator's own logic or prompt.
- **no**: neither.

## Derived, not rated
Directory (the service) = **yes** if C3 and C4 are both yes; **part** if either is yes or part; **no** if both are no.
